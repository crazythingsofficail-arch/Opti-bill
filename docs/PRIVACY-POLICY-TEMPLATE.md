PRIVACY NOTICE

Who handles your information

{{legal_name}}, trading as {{name}}, operates this optical shop at {{address}}, {{city}}, {{state}} {{pincode}}. The shop decides why and how the customer records described here are used and is the responsible data fiduciary/operator. You can contact the shop at {{phone}} or {{email}}. Opti-X Billing is the software used to organise these records; product support is available at hello@opti-x.in.

What we record

For an optical purchase or eye test, we may record your name and mobile number; an alternative contact; gender if you choose to provide it; age or date of birth to determine whether a guardian is needed; contact/address details; and a business GSTIN where applicable. We also record vision-test measurements, prescription history, examiner details, fitting measurements, relevant clinical remarks, frame and lens choices, an optional lens-packet photograph, order progress and promised delivery dates. Invoices and payment records include amounts, dates, payment mode and a transaction reference. We do not ask you to enter card numbers, PINs, banking passwords or Aadhaar numbers into this system.

Why we use it

We use the records to provide the requested optical service, prepare a prescription, make or fit your selected products, coordinate essential lab work, fulfil orders, contact you about collection or unpaid amounts, support warranty/return requests and keep required accounting records. We do not use clinical records for behavioural advertising or unrelated marketing. A separate, specific permission would be needed for a new optional purpose.

Your choice and consent

Before a new record is saved, staff explain the notice and request consent. The system records the consent wording, notice version and time. You may decline optional information. If you decline information necessary to carry out a particular service safely or to issue a required invoice, we will explain the effect on that service. You can withdraw optional consent by contacting the grievance contact below or visiting the shop. Withdrawal does not undo completed lawful processing or remove records that must be retained for a legal obligation.

Children

For anyone under 18, staff require guardian details, an affirmative guardian consent record and a note of how the guardian’s identity and authority were verified. Staff must actually complete verification; ticking a box is not a substitute. The software does not ask staff to copy government identity numbers. Guardians can use the same request channels for the child’s records, subject to identity and authority checks.

Who may receive information

Authorised shop staff and optometrists have role-limited access. A lab may receive the prescription and job details needed to fulfil the order. Payment providers process payments through their own systems; the shop records only the payment mode, amount and reference. An operator may share an invoice through WhatsApp at your request or when appropriately authorised. A PDF share link can include your prescription and photo, can be forwarded by its recipient, and expires after one hour. Ask for a printed copy if you prefer not to use that channel. Necessary infrastructure providers process data for hosting, authentication, storage and email delivery. Disclosure to an authority is limited to a valid legal requirement.

Storage and safeguards

The intended deployment uses Supabase cloud infrastructure in Mumbai, India. The shop must confirm its actual project region and separately review where application hosting, email, backups and any optional support tools process information. Access uses authenticated sessions and shop-specific database policies. Images are private and delivered with short-lived URLs. Financial records and audit entries are protected against ordinary editing. Backups and operational security are managed by the shop and its authorised deployment operator. No system can promise absolute security; staff should report suspected misuse promptly.

How long records are kept

The shop’s configured clinical-record review period is {{clinical_retention_months}} months. We review whether records are still needed for ongoing service, disputes or another lawful purpose and act on verified requests. This setting does not trigger indiscriminate automated deletion. Accounting retention has a configured floor of {{retention_months}} months, measured using the relevant statutory starting point. For GST records this is linked to the annual-return due date, rather than simply the invoice date, and proceedings can require longer retention. We keep only necessary invoice identity when contact and clinical records are erased. Private backups follow a controlled retention and restoration process; erased information must not be reintroduced into ordinary use from an old backup.

Access, correction, erasure and concerns

You may ask for information about your records, a copy, correction of inaccurate information, or erasure of information no longer needed. You may also ask about withdrawal of consent and nomination of another person to exercise applicable rights in the circumstances provided by law. We verify the requester’s identity and authority without collecting unnecessary documents, record the request and explain any legal reason we cannot fully comply. A clinical correction is made transparently; issued tax documents are corrected using the accounting workflow. Requests are handled within the periods required by the law in force. Applicable statutory rights and complaint routes depend on their notified commencement and any relevant exceptions; this notice does not reduce them.

Grievance and incident response

Contact {{grievance_name}} at {{grievance_email}}, write to the shop address above, or raise the issue in person. Product support can be reached at hello@opti-x.in and will route shop-specific issues to the responsible operator. We investigate concerns, restrict unauthorised access and preserve necessary incident evidence. Where an incident triggers legal notice duties, the responsible operator will notify affected people and the relevant authority as required by the applicable rules and time limits.

Changes to this notice

The displayed version and last-updated date identify the current notice. We retain published versions and record the version used when consent is captured. A materially new optional purpose requires a fresh explanation and appropriate consent.
