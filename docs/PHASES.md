## Design system

Roboto is bundled locally and loaded through next/font. The supplied Latin and Latin-extended subsets were combined to preserve the Indian rupee symbol in browser and PDF output; font licenses are included. Light tokens are white / #F5F5F7 / #E5E5EA / #0A0A0A / #6E6E73. Dark tokens are black / #1C1C1E / #2C2C2E / #F5F5F7 / #98989D. Yellow #FACC15 is the accent. The signature KPI card positions white text on its dark region.

Inputs have 12 px radii, cards 20 px and sheets 28 px. Navigation uses translucent blur; motion respects reduced-motion preference. Focus rings, associated labels, live toasts, dialog semantics, keyboard shortcuts, skeletons, error states and empty states are shared components. Mobile navigation uses a prominent New Bill control; tables adapt to cards, and long prescription grids retain explicit horizontal scrolling.

## Money and workflow decisions

- Tax-exclusive unit prices; positive whole quantities with a user-entered unit label.
- Line discounts first; overall flat/percentage discount then allocated using largest remainders in stable line order.
- GST rates are integer basis points, with equal half-rates for CGST/SGST. Each tax component is rounded to paise. The invoice rounds to whole rupees at the end with explicit round-off.
- Stock decreases only on successful finalisation. Owner-selected physical return can restore stock on a cancellation/full credit, exactly once.
- Payment corrections append opposite-signed rows; no payment is edited/deleted. A fully credited bill shows refund due until recorded payments are reversed as appropriate.
- The user request to lock finalised rows conflicts with updating their paid/balance columns. This implementation resolves it with an immutable invoice document plus a separate ledger projection and cancellation-event table. The public register presents a coherent combined result.
- Erasure cannot be one atomic SQL transaction across Postgres and remote Storage. A transactional outbox makes removal durable and visibly pending until confirmed by the storage API.
