# Third-party software and fonts

Dependencies are listed and locked in package.json / package-lock.json. Their own licenses apply. Review the dependency licenses with your deployment policy before distribution.

Roboto is obtained from the @fontsource/roboto package. The original font license is included at public/fonts/ROBOTO-LICENSE.txt. Latin and Latin-extended subsets are combined into the local Roboto fonts so U+20B9 (Indian rupee) is available in both browser and PDF output. No letterforms were redrawn.

The icons in the UI come from lucide-react. The small app mark in public/icons is a code-drawn spectacles symbol.
