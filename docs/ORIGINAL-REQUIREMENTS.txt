## 1. PRODUCT VISION
Opti-X Billing is a premium, Apple-grade billing and records system for independent
optical shops in India. A counter staff member must be able to take a walk-in
customer from "name and phone" to "printed GST invoice with prescription" in under
2 minutes on a phone or desktop.

Users and roles:
- Owner: full access, settings, reports, cancel/credit-note, user management, data erasure.
- Staff: create customers, prescriptions, orders, invoices and payments. Cannot cancel
  invoices, change settings, export data or delete data.
- Optometrist (optional role): create and edit prescriptions only.

Primary devices: Android phone (most common), desktop with A4 printer, 80mm thermal printer.
Languages: English UI. Design the text layer so Kannada and Hindi can be added later (i18n-ready).

## 5. DATABASE DESIGN (Supabase Postgres)
Conventions: uuid primary keys (gen_random_uuid), shop_id on every business table,
created_at / updated_at (trigger-maintained), created_by, deleted_at (soft delete),
money in integer paise, citext or lower-case indexes for search.

Tables and key columns:
- shops: name, legal_name, gstin, pan (optional), address lines, city, state, state_code,
  pincode, phone, email, logo_path, gst_scheme (regular | composition | unregistered),
  invoice_prefix, financial_year_start_month (default 4), terms_text, warranty_text,
  return_policy_text, upi_id, bank details (optional), signature_path.
- profiles: id (= auth.users.id), shop_id, full_name, role (owner|staff|optometrist), is_active.
- customers: name, phone (E.164 +91), alt_phone, gender (male|female|other|undisclosed),
  dob or age_years, email, address, city, notes, consent_given (bool), consent_at,
  consent_version, anonymised_at. Unique (shop_id, phone) for active rows.
- prescriptions: customer_id, test_date, examiner_name, source (in_shop|external),
  for each eye (od_ / os_ prefix): sph, cyl, axis, add_power, prism, base, pd_distance,
  pd_near, seg_height, va_unaided, va_aided. Plus bin_pd, vertex_distance, pantoscopic_tilt,
  remarks, valid_until. Constraints: SPH/CYL in -30.00 to +30.00 step 0.25, AXIS 1-180
  required if CYL is non-zero, ADD 0.25 to 4.00.
- frames (inventory): sku/model_no, brand, colour, size (eye-bridge-temple), material,
  type (full rim|half rim|rimless), gender_target, cost_paise, mrp_paise, stock_qty.
- orders: customer_id, prescription_id, order_no, status, lab_name, lab_sent_at,
  promised_date, delivered_at, notes.
- order_items: order_id, item_type (frame|lens|contact_lens|sunglass|accessory|service|other),
  description, frame_model_no, lens_brand, lens_code, lens_type (single vision|bifocal|
  progressive|office), lens_material/index, coating(s), tint, qty, unit_price_paise,
  discount_paise, tax_category_id.
- uploads: order_id/customer_id, storage_path, mime, size_bytes, kind (lens_photo),
  uploaded_by. One lens image per order enforced by a unique partial index.
- tax_categories: name (Frames, Lenses, Sunglasses, Contact Lenses, Accessories,
  Services), hsn_code, gst_rate_bp (basis points), is_active, effective_from.
  Rates are rows, not code, and have effective dates so past invoices stay correct.
- invoices: invoice_no, financial_year, series_seq, type (tax_invoice|bill_of_supply|
  credit_note), status (draft|finalised|cancelled), customer_id, order_id,
  customer_snapshot jsonb and shop_snapshot jsonb (frozen at finalisation, so
  later edits never change old invoices), place_of_supply_state_code, is_interstate,
  subtotal, discount_total, taxable_total, cgst, sgst, igst, round_off, grand_total
  (all paise), amount_paid, balance_due, payment_status, finalised_at, cancelled_at,
  cancel_reason, original_invoice_id (for credit notes).
- invoice_items: snapshot of each line (description, hsn, qty, rate, discount, taxable
  value, cgst/sgst/igst rate and amount).
- payments: invoice_id, amount_paise, mode (cash|upi|card|bank|other), reference_no,
  paid_at, received_by. Payment rows are append-only; corrections are made with reversing rows.
- invoice_counters: shop_id, financial_year, prefix, last_seq (atomic increment).
- audit_logs: actor_id, entity, entity_id, action, old_data, new_data, ip, at.
  Append-only, with no update or delete allowed for anyone.
- data_requests: customer_id, type (access|correction|erasure), status, handled_by, handled_at.

Database logic (in SQL, not in the app):
- next_invoice_no(shop, fy): atomic and gap-free per series, format PREFIX/YY-YY/0001,
  max 16 characters, only A-Z 0-9 / and -.
- Trigger: block UPDATE/DELETE on finalised invoices and invoice_items.
- Trigger: write to audit_logs on invoices, payments, customers, settings and tax_categories.
- Trigger: recompute amount_paid and balance_due from payments.
- RPC finalise_invoice(): in one transaction, validate, snapshot, assign the number, lock.
- RPC erase_customer(): anonymise the personal fields, delete the storage images, keep
  invoice-required tax data as the law requires, and log the request.

Row Level Security: enabled on every table. Policy pattern: shop_id = (select shop_id
from profiles where id = auth.uid()). Role checks per action (staff cannot cancel, change
settings or export). Storage bucket "lens-images" is private, path {shop_id}/{customer_id}/{uuid}.webp,
with policies mirroring table access. Serve images through signed URLs that expire in 60 seconds.

## 6. NEW BILL WIZARD (core workflow)
Step 1, Customer: search by phone first. If found, show the profile and last visit. If
not found, show an inline create form (name, phone, gender, age/DOB). The consent
checkbox is required for new customers.
Step 2, Prescription: reuse the latest or add new. Layout is an OD/OS grid with
steppers in 0.25 increments, +/- sign toggles, auto-formatting (+1.25, -0.75), PD, ADD,
and remarks. Show warnings for unusual values, never silent changes.
Step 3, Items: add frame (pick from inventory or type a model number), lens (brand,
code, type, index, coating), and extras. Upload or capture ONE lens photo (client
compress to <=300 KB webp, preview, replace/remove). Per-line discount and an
overall discount (flat or %).
Step 4, Payment and Review: live totals with GST breakup, advance amount, payment mode
(split allowed), promised delivery date, order notes. Buttons: Save as Draft, Finalise
and Print.
Drafts autosave. Back/forward never loses data. Duplicate-bill guard on double-tap.

## 7. INDIAN GST AND BILLING RULES (configurable, verify with a CA)
- Tax invoice must carry: supplier name, address, GSTIN; consecutive unique invoice
  number (<=16 chars, FY-wise series); date; recipient name (and address and state for
  B2B or when required); HSN per line (digits configurable, 4 or 6); description; qty;
  unit; taxable value; discount; CGST+SGST for intra-state or IGST for inter-state
  (decided from place of supply vs shop state); rate and amount of each tax; total in
  figures AND in words; place of supply; signature block; "Reverse charge: No".
- Optional customer GSTIN for B2B. Validate the GSTIN format and checksum.
- Composition or unregistered shops: issue "Bill of Supply", with no tax lines and
  the mandatory composition declaration text.
- Tax calculation engine: pure, unit-tested functions. Rounding rule: compute per
  line, sum, then round the invoice total to the nearest rupee with a visible Round Off line.
- Discounts reduce the taxable value before tax.
- Finalised invoices cannot be edited. Corrections go through Cancel (owner, with
  reason) or Credit Note (linked to the original) and a fresh invoice.
- The invoice series never reuses or skips numbers except for cancelled ones, which stay visible.
- Retention: keep invoice and accounting records for the legally required period
  (GST law requires 6 years); this is configurable and documented in the Privacy Policy.
- Add an on-screen note in Settings: "Tax rates and HSN codes change. Confirm current
  rates with your Chartered Accountant or the GST portal before use."
- Include a feature flag and architecture hooks for e-invoicing (IRN/QR) if the shop
  crosses the notified turnover limit. Do not implement the IRP integration yet.
